# Video Business To-Do List

## Niche Research Websites

Use these tools one at a time to find and validate YouTube niches:

- [ ] AnswerThePublic - discover questions and topics people search for
- [ ] Exploding Topics - find topics and trends that are growing
- [ ] Google Trends - compare interest over time and by location
- [ ] vidIQ - research YouTube keywords, competition, and video ideas
- [ ] Ubersuggest - check keywords, search volume, and related topics

## Workflow

1. Start with AnswerThePublic and write down 5 to 10 recurring questions.
2. Check those topics in Exploding Topics for growth potential.
3. Compare the strongest topics in Google Trends.
4. Use vidIQ to check YouTube competition and keyword opportunities.
5. Use Ubersuggest to confirm search volume and related keywords.
6. Choose one focused topic for the first video.
7. Add the chosen idea to the app's Calendar & ideas section.
8. Generate and review the first video before uploading it manually to YouTube.
